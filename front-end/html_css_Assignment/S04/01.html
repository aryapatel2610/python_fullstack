My Amazing Trip to matheran

Travelled on July 21, 2025

matheran is one of the most beautiful cities in Maharashtra, famous for its Scenic Viewpoints, The Toy Train, and amazing green mountains.

During my trip, I visited City Echo Point, Charlotte Lake, Louisa Point, and enjoyed the beautiful sunset near the lake.

Places I Visited

  • Echo Point
  • Charlotte Lake
  • Louisa Point
  • Toy Train

My Experience

The trip was an unforgettable experience. The beautiful views, and peaceful atmosphere made my journey very special.
